Tax years
Per-account, per-year tax computations — draft until you finalize and file.
Computations are versioned. A year starts as DRAFT and can be recalculated freely as underlying transactions, corporate actions, or FX rates change. Once you file it, it becomes FINALIZED and is locked — any later change to source data produces a new draft version rather than silently altering the filed result.
